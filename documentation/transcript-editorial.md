## Reading edition of 18 October 2026

The base text is the author's edited Word reading edition published on 17 October 2026. That edition remains available in the [repository history](https://github.com/ComputerLars/Syntheticism/blob/8f1a8ed61664f3ce379f77b01353d3bcef12375a/transcript/Syntheticism-Defence-21-September-2026-Reading-Edition.docx).

Successive paragraphs by the same speaker now form one turn. A new paragraph does not in itself introduce another speaker label. Paragraph breaks that divided the same sentence have been joined; the other paragraph boundaries remain. Actual changes of speaker, interruptions and existing uncertainty markers are retained.

The opening remarks and lecture belong to recording 1, as does the discussion with Jan Bäcklund. Recording 2 begins after the break and contains the discussions with Sine Nørholm Just and Jussi Parikka. Timestamps restart for recording 2. Every original timestamp remains an addressable passage anchor, including those whose repeated speaker labels are no longer displayed. The complete mapping is in [navigation.json](../transcript/navigation.json).

Numbered dissertation articles are written as *Article 1*, *Article 2*, and so on. Collective references use *Articles 1 and 2*, for example. Generic mentions of articles, newspapers and scholarship are unchanged.

Links and numbered references are editorial additions. Article links lead to the corresponding article leaf in the complete dissertation. The passage references distinguish an article's complete extent from a particular figure or synthesis discussion. Explicit page references in the speech retain their wording and link to the cited page. Numbers refer to printed folios; the PDF viewer is one page ahead. Thus printed p. 54 opens PDF page 55.

The Word and Markdown versions contain the same spoken text. The editorial work described here does not re-transcribe the recordings or replace spoken formulations with the dissertation's wording. Existing bracketed uncertainties remain visible. The source recordings are not included in this repository.
