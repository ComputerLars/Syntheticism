# PhD defence of Asker Bryld Staunæs

## English

On **Monday 21 September 2026, 13:15–16:15 CEST**, **Asker Bryld Staunæs** defended *Syntheticism: How I Learned to Love Democracy*, an article-based PhD dissertation in artistic research, at **Aarhus University**. The public defence took place in **Lille Sal, Kasernen, Building 1585/119**, Langelandsgade 143, 8000 Aarhus C, Denmark.

The assessment committee comprised **Professor Jussi Parikka** (Aarhus University, chair), **Professor Sine Nørholm Just** (Roskilde University), and **Senior Lecturer Jan Bäcklund** (Linnaeus University). The supervisors were **Associate Professor Lotte Philipsen** and **Professor Cristina Maria Flesher Fominaya**, Aarhus University. **Stefan Iversen** led the proceedings. The PhD degree was awarded on **6 October 2026**.

The research was supported by **the Novo Nordisk Foundation**, through a **Mads Øvlisen PhD Scholarship in practice-based research in art**, grant **NNF23OC0081575**, at Aarhus University and Kunsthal Aarhus, **2023–2026**.

## Dansk

**Mandag den 21. september 2026 kl. 13.15–16.15** forsvarede **Asker Bryld Staunæs** *Syntheticism: How I Learned to Love Democracy*, en artikelbaseret ph.d.-afhandling i kunstnerisk forskning, ved **Aarhus Universitet**. Det offentlige forsvar fandt sted i **Lille Sal, Kasernen, bygning 1585/119**, Langelandsgade 143, 8000 Aarhus C.

Bedømmelsesudvalget bestod af **professor Jussi Parikka** (Aarhus Universitet, forperson), **professor Sine Nørholm Just** (Roskilde Universitet) og **universitetslektor Jan Bäcklund** (Linnéuniversitetet). Vejlederne var **lektor Lotte Philipsen** og **professor Cristina Maria Flesher Fominaya**, Aarhus Universitet. **Stefan Iversen** ledede forsvarshandlingen. Ph.d.-graden blev tildelt **6. oktober 2026**.

Forskningen var støttet af **Novo Nordisk Fonden** gennem et **Mads Øvlisen ph.d.-stipendium i praksisbaseret kunstforskning**, bevilling **NNF23OC0081575**, ved Aarhus Universitet og Kunsthal Aarhus, **2023–2026**.
